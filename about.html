About Ridho Utama Berkah

An Indonesian export company connecting local potential with global opportunity.

Ridho Utama Berkah focuses on coffee, spices, agricultural by-products, and bamboo handicrafts for international buyers who value responsive service and clear export preparation.

Company Profile

Exporting Indonesian products with professional communication and practical preparation.

We work to bridge Indonesian supply with international demand by preparing product information, discussing buyer requirements, arranging suitable packaging, and coordinating shipment readiness.

Our product focus includes Arabica and Robusta coffee, selected spices, rice-based agricultural by-products, and bamboo handicrafts. Each inquiry is handled based on product availability, requested specification, quantity, destination, and market requirements.

We aim to become a dependable partner for importers, wholesalers, distributors, roasters, ingredient buyers, and retailers looking for Indonesian export products.

What we provide

  • Product sourcing and availability discussion
  • Specification alignment before order confirmation
  • Export packing options for bulk and retail needs
  • Shipment coordination support for international buyers
Vision

To become a trusted Indonesian export partner recognized for quality, reliability, and long-term buyer relationships.

Mission
  • Promote Indonesian coffee, spices, natural products, and handicrafts to international markets.
  • Prepare products through responsible sorting, processing, packing, and shipment coordination.
  • Build transparent communication with buyers from inquiry to repeat orders.
  • Support local supply chains by helping Indonesian products access global opportunities.

Brand Story

Brewing blessings in every bean.

Our brand identity represents global connection, Indonesian origin, and the commitment to deliver natural products with care.

From Indonesian sources to world markets

We combine commodity focus, export awareness, and friendly buyer communication to create a smoother sourcing experience for international customers.
